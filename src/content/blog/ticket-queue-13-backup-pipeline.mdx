<p><span class="c-light">The ticket:</span> logadmin1 lost their backup script when the previous admin left. Three things to rebuild. A daily compressed tarball of <code>/etc</code>, a weekly bzip2 snapshot of <code>/home/dev1</code> for better compression on source code, and a continuous rsync mirror of <code>/var/log</code> that excludes already-rotated <code>.gz</code> files and removes anything from the mirror that is gone from the source. Also test scp by copying one archive locally.</p>

<p><span class="c-light">The real problem:</span> two traps live here. The trailing slash in rsync changes what gets copied, and the <code>--delete</code> flag is unforgiving. Both deserve a dry run.</p>

<p><span class="c-light">What we are doing:</span> tar in every compression flavor, rsync with delete and exclude, and scp.</p>

<p>The three compressors trade speed for ratio. gzip is fast and moderate, good for daily backups. bzip2 is slower but compresses text and source better. xz is slowest and smallest, for long-term storage.</p>

<h2 id="tar-etc" class="pp-h2">Daily archive of /etc</h2>

<p>Put the date in the filename so daily runs do not overwrite each other. <code>c z v f</code> means create, gzip, verbose, filename. Always verify the archive after.</p>

<h2 id="rsync" class="pp-h2">The rsync mirror and the trailing slash</h2>

<p>The trailing slash on the source is the whole game. A trailing slash copies the contents of the directory. No trailing slash copies the directory itself into the destination. Because <code>--delete</code> removes anything in the destination that is not in the source, always dry run first.</p>

<div class="pp-code"><div class="pp-code-head"><div class="pp-code-dots"><div class="pp-code-dot"></div><div class="pp-code-dot"></div><div class="pp-code-dot"></div></div><div class="pp-code-lang">bash</div><button class="pp-code-copy" onclick="navigator.clipboard.writeText(this.closest('.pp-code').querySelector('pre').textContent);this.textContent='copied!';setTimeout(()=>this.textContent='copy',1500)">copy</button></div><div class="pp-code-body"><pre>{`# the difference, in words:\n#   rsync -av /src/  /dst/   copies the CONTENTS of src into dst\n#   rsync -av /src   /dst/   creates /dst/src/\n\n# dry run first, always, with --delete\nsudo rsync -av --dry-run --delete --exclude='*.gz' /var/log/ /srv/backups/logs/\n\n# looks safe? run it for real\nsudo rsync -av --delete --exclude='*.gz' /var/log/ /srv/backups/logs/\ndu -sh /srv/backups/logs/`}</pre></div></div>
