privacy & data

// what happens to your data when you visit

Short version: this is a static blog. I don't run ads, I don't sell anything, and I don't want your data.

what gets collected
  • Server logs. This site is hosted on Cloudflare, which records IP address, browser type, and requested page for every visit. That's standard infrastructure logging — it's how the site gets served and how abuse gets blocked. I don't have a way to turn it off, and I don't personally look at it.
  • Contact form submissions. If you message me, I get whatever you typed — name, email, message. I keep it in my inbox so I can reply.
what does not get collected
  • No analytics. No Google Analytics, no Plausible, no pixel, no heatmaps.
  • No tracking cookies. No advertising cookies. No cross-site tracking.
  • No accounts, no logins, no profiles.
  • No newsletter list — there isn't one yet.

who else sees it

Cloudflare hosts the site and therefore processes request logs on my behalf. Google Fonts serves the typeface, which means your browser makes a request to Google and Google sees your IP. That's the only third party the page reaches out to. Nobody else gets anything, and I don't sell, share, or trade any of it.

how long I keep it

Cloudflare's logs roll off on their retention schedule, not mine. Contact messages stay in my inbox until I clear them out — if you'd rather I delete yours sooner, ask and I will.

your choices

  • Ask me what I have about you, and I'll tell you.
  • Ask me to delete it, and I will.
  • Read the site without contacting me, and there's nothing tied to you beyond an infrastructure log line.

changes

If I add anything that touches your data — analytics, a newsletter, comments — I'll update this page and date it. This page is in the site's git history, so you can see exactly what changed and when.
